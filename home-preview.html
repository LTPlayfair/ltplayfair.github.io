BRUTALIST
FIGURATION.

Raw, direct, and unfiltered. An instinctive approach to drawing where the character and imperfections of the original marks remain visible. Formed in the Cancel Culture Years.

Canadian artist based in West Vancouver, shaped by time in Amsterdam and London. The work has appeared on Times Square billboards in New York.
